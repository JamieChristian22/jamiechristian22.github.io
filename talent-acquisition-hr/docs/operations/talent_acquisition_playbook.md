# Enterprise Talent Acquisition: end-to-end operating playbook

**Company:** Northstar People & Talent (fictional) • **Effective scenario date:** 2026-10-31 • **Scope:** requisition-to-start for U.S.-based job openings • **Process owner:** Head of Talent Acquisition • **Escalation:** HR Director / Legal / Privacy team, where appropriate.

## 1. Govern requisition intake before sourcing

**Trigger:** Business unit requests approved headcount or replacement. **Inputs:** validated headcount slot, job-related qualifications, location and work arrangement, funding/compensation range, classification, interview panel, target start date, accessibility and candidate experience considerations. **Outputs:** completed requisition in ATS and written scorecard rubric; posting approval. **RACI:** hiring manager accountable for requirements, TA manages workflow, Finance approves funding, HRBP validates level/pay band, Legal reviews special requirements.

1. Require business justification, funding owner, approval date, job family/level, manager and recruiter assignment. Block public posting without written budget authorization.
2. Separate **must-have job capabilities** from teachable and merely preferred skills. Reject pedigree proxies unless validated as job-related.
3. Confirm salary band against internal compensation architecture, applicable pay transparency laws and internal equity checks (jurisdiction-specific review).
4. Confirm work arrangement, work authorization language (without discriminatory documentary preference), availability, reasonable accommodation contact and alternative assessment formats.
5. Establish interview rubric **before** first screening decision; restrict rubric edits once candidates are assessed unless change is controlled and applied consistently.
6. Set hiring decision meeting, communications cadence, and individual stage owner.
7. Document business exceptions; retain approval log rather than changing requisition facts silently.

**Deliverable:** `templates/operations/req_intake_completed_example.md`. **Quality gate:** Approved req, role rubric, panel and interview process ready.

## 2. Job architecture and accessible posting

Write business-impact summary and realistic responsibilities. Use essential functions, relevant tools/competencies, range and classification as approved. Avoid exclusionary phrases, age proxies, unnecessary degree/license demands and accessibility barriers. Separate minimum from preferred requirements. Include neutral EEO and accommodations notice after company legal/HR approval. Keep an accessible text version of the job description and record publishing channels.

**Check:** hiring manager signs off on duties, HRBP on level/comp, TA on candidate experience, Legal on jurisdiction-specific claims.

## 3. Sourcing campaign and candidate outreach

**Cadence (fictional SOP):** daily application triage; two documented sourcing blocks per week per critical req; weekly sourcing mix review. Use approved channels, contact consent/preferences and documented opt-out. Do not scrape private personal data or auto-contact people. Record source consistently; no invented response rates. For initial outreach, describe work arrangement, real role scope and candidate choice; avoid unsubstantiated salary promises. Track each candidate only via ATS ID and permitted business fields.

- Process direct applicants in submission order or approved objective workflow.
- Use clearly job-related screening criteria; human review ambiguous evidence.
- Offer reasonable accommodations and accessible formats without requesting medical details from candidates.
- Avoid connecting volunteer demographic responses to candidate view or interview selection.
- Honor contact opt-outs and remove expired personal data per approved schedule.

**Deliverable:** source plan / contact cadence / candidate status audit trail. **QA:** no candidate record missing req, owner, source or current stage.

## 4. Screening and interviewing

**Screening gate:** applicant meets validated objective minimums and confirms job-relevant details; do not use an automated hidden suitability score. **Interview scheduling:** send role, expected duration, process format, accessible accommodation route and rescheduling channel. Assign trained panelists and conflict-of-interest check.

Structured interview: (1) ask the same core role-relevant questions for applicants at the same stage; (2) document actual observed evidence and anchored ratings; (3) allow follow-ups for clarification; (4) record evidence before group discussion; (5) calibrate with the panel and document tradeoffs. Panelists should not access voluntarily submitted demographic forms or unrelated personal information. **Job-relatedness, consistency and reasonable accommodations are mandatory review topics**, not optional scoring features.

**Artifacts:** `templates/interviews/structured_interview_kit.md`, `templates/interviews/completed_scorecard_example.md`, and operational workbook's Scorecard tab. **Escalate:** accommodation request, potential discrimination, panel conflict, unverifiable requirement or inconsistent evaluation.

## 5. Decision, disposition and experience

1. Record interview evidence and rubric-aligned judgment, rather than personal similarities or cultural-fit shorthand.
2. Obtain documented hiring-manager decision, recruiter validation, and any required pay/level approval.
3. Notify applicants promptly under local policy and keep candidate communication respectful; never imply a person failed a medical/background check unless a legally correct process was followed.
4. Use non-discriminatory, job-related disposition codes. Avoid writing subjective or unnecessary protected/private details in the ATS.
5. Analyze stage conversion and candidate experience in aggregate; review negative impact signals with compliance specialists instead of using group data for candidate selection.

## 6. Offer approval and closing

**Before extending:** approved requisition and pay, level and location verification, recruiter and HRBP approval, conditional steps where lawful, candidate contact channel, offer version and deadline. Never include actual offer or personal documents in a public repo. Provide clear, accessible compensation details. Obtain legally required disclosures and permissions **before** consumer-report background checks; handle any pre-/post-adverse action through the approved FCRA/legal workflow (see governance guide). Keep background reports outside standard recruiting analytics.

**Offer KPI:** Accepted / (Accepted + Declined), pending excluded. Track decline reason carefully and voluntary category-level feedback; do not treat a single small cell as causal evidence.

## 7. Preboarding and onboarding handoff

After accepted offer and confirmed hire: record new employee ID, start date and responsible HR/IT manager, provide equipment plan, schedule welcome, initiate HRIS/policy/benefits, prepare performance goals, and route I-9 completion to the **authorized compliant process**. The project records an *I-9 process handoff* task only; it does not store identity documents or create official I-9 records. Track tasks by owner/due date. Address overdue work in daily standup and flag start-blocking tasks to HR Ops and IT.

## 8. Weekly management control meeting (30 minutes)

- **Before meeting:** validate req changes, open aging, late interviews, candidates awaiting response, pending offers, backlogged onboarding, HR case risks.
- **0–5 min:** demand changes, approved capacity and hiring priorities.
- **5–15 min:** blocked requisitions, stage bottlenecks, candidate experience service breaches.
- **15–20 min:** offers, compensation approvals, background process dependencies.
- **20–25 min:** new starters, equipment/HRIS handoffs and overdue items.
- **25–30 min:** commitments, accountable owner, due date, risk log and decision register.

**Escalation levels:** SLA at risk -> assigned owner same day; critical candidate accommodation/privacy problem -> HR/Legal immediately; unresolved cross-team blocker -> Director in next management cadence. Communications should be professional and documented without exposing sensitive details in an unapproved system.

## 9. Controls and sample acceptance criteria

- Every requisition has approved funding before publication.
- Every interview uses consistent written criteria with observable evidence.
- Every formal offer has documented approval and decision status.
- Every hire has manager/HR/IT onboarding ownership; milestone completion timestamps are auditable.
- Every applicant disposition uses a documented job-related reason category.
- Every metric can be reconstructed from transaction/event tables with transparent denominator and snapshot.
- Every privacy-sensitive issue is escalated and restricted from general recruiter access.

## 10. Role readiness

This playbook demonstrates process knowledge applicable to recruiting coordinator, TA specialist, sourcing coordinator, recruiting operations analyst, HR operations associate, HRIS reporting analyst and people analytics entry-level roles. It is not evidence of actual employment in one of those positions.
