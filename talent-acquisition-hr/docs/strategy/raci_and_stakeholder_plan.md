# Ownership, RACI and stakeholder communications

Key: **R** Responsible (does work), **A** Accountable (decision owner), **C** Consulted, **I** Informed. One A per activity where practical. Role titles are hypothetical.

| Activity | Hiring Manager | Recruiter | TA Ops | HRBP / Comp | HR Ops | IT | Legal / Privacy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headcount & job requirements | A/R | C | I | C | I | I | I |
| Requisition publishing | C | R | A | C | I | I | C |
| Sourcing, consent & candidate comms | I | A/R | C | I | I | I | C |
| Structured interview & decision | A/R | R | C | C | I | I | C |
| Offer pay / level approval | C | R | C | A | I | I | C |
| FCRA/background compliance process | I | C | I | C | R | I | A |
| HRIS & onboarding coordination | C | C | I | C | A/R | R | C |
| HR service request lifecycle | I | I | I | C | A/R | C | C |
| Metric certification, dashboard QA | C | C | A/R | C | C | I | C |
| Privacy/record retention & AI use review | I | C | C | C | C | C | A/R |

**Dependencies:** TA cannot publish without approved headcount/comp; HR cannot validate onboarding before a confirmed hire; IT cannot grant access without manager/HR authorization. Metric release cannot proceed if quality validation fails.

### Standard stakeholder updates

- **Daily recruiter queue:** interviews to schedule, candidate response pending, active offer deadlines; owner + next action.
- **Weekly hiring manager check-in:** req age, stage movement, pipeline quality concerns, decisions needed, candidate experience.
- **Weekly HR Ops standup:** starts in next two weeks, late IT tasks, HR case backlog, urgent specialists.
- **Monthly leadership review:** staffing demand, fill/hire cycle, offer status, HR service reliability, risks and accountable actions.
- **Quarterly governance:** policy and retention review, selection validation, data access recertification, metric dictionary changes and adverse impact signals for qualified specialists.

**Escalation rules:** privacy leak, prohibited interview question or accommodation barrier -> immediate Legal/HR review; blocked new-hire access within 2 business days of start -> HR Ops + IT owner same day; offer authorization gap -> hold issuance; overdue business-critical req -> TA leadership prioritization; data quality failure -> stop dashboard release.
