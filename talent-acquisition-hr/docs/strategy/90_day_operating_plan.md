## Hypothesis test design

**Offer funnel:** pre-register offer acceptance = accepted / decided, with sample n and time window. Segment by job family and compensation band only when minimum cell sizes protect privacy. Compare a mature offer cohort before/after operational change, not all offers across different observation windows. Inspect decline reason completeness and external market changes; do not assume cause.

**Onboarding:** each required task has an owner, due date and completion evidence. Track on-time tasks / all due tasks with separate overdue-open count. Use new-hire-level completion as a secondary measure and check late task severity.

**HR support:** track open overdue case count, aging distribution, resolved-within-policy fraction and reopened rate (not modeled yet). Preserve sensitive case segregation; not all tickets have identical lawful urgency.

## Governance cadence and stop conditions

- Weekly operations: review unresolved blockers, owner commitments, data quality and candidate communication.
- Biweekly TA + HRBP + Legal: review accommodations and selection consistency issues without candidate-identifying analytics exports.
- Monthly leadership: review baseline compared with applicable targets and explain denominator limits.
- Roll back any procedure that worsens access control, creates unvalidated exclusion, increases applicant delays materially or breaches required approvals.
- A proposal is complete only after someone signs a decision record with owner, date, evidence and exception rationale.

## Project success definition

Achievement is **a testable operating system with governance**, not a promised numerical outcome. Treat all changes as hypotheses until independently measured.
