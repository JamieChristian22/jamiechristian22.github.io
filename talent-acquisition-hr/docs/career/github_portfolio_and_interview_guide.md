## Sample STAR answer (present clearly as an independent portfolio case)

**Situation:** I created a fictional corporate recruiting and HR Operations scenario to practice end-to-end reporting and process controls.

**Task:** I wanted to make the dataset and outputs consistent, build a recruiter/HR workflow and identify realistic operational priorities.

**Action:** I wrote Python to generate deterministic ATS and HRIS records, modeled relational constraints in SQLite, defined recruiting and people service KPIs, built dashboards and an Excel workbook, wrote selection/access policies and added automated quality tests. I then created an executive improvement roadmap with owners and governance checks.

**Result:** The end-to-end demonstration is reproducible and validated for its synthetic data. It identifies offer and onboarding process problems as hypotheses for a fictional leadership team. It does not assert real employer outcomes or an improvement that was never measured.
