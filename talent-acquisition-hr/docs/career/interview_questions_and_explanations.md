# Job-ready Talent Acquisition / HR Operations interview prep

Answers below are demonstration talking points grounded in the **independent synthetic portfolio**, not claims of professional employment at a staffing company.

## 1. Walk me through a requisition from approval to hire.

Describe the intake request, approved headcount/pay, essential job-related competencies, accessible posting, sourcing, ATS stage updates, consistent structured interview, human debrief, offer approval, candidate communication, lawful conditions, HRIS onboarding handoff and reporting. A useful quality principle: every stage must have a documented owner and accurate timestamp.

## 2. What is the difference between time-to-fill and time-to-hire?

Time-to-fill is the calendar days from requisition opening to successful closure for filled requisitions. Time-to-hire is from a hired applicant's application date to the hire event. In this demo, time-to-fill averages 56.2 days across 24 filled reqs, while hired applications average 30.0 days. Those populations are different.

## 3. How is offer acceptance rate calculated?

Accepted offers divided by all decided offers (accepted + declined); exclude pending. The fictional case is 24 accepted among 77 decided = 31.2%, with 12 pending excluded. A poor rate triggers follow-up on approval timing, candidate alignment, role and comp expectations but is not itself proof of cause.

## 4. What is an ATS versus HRIS?

An ATS manages requisitions, applicant workflows, interviews, offers and disposition records. An HRIS/HCM system manages employee master data, joins/moves/exits and authorized people-related workflows. A secure connection should preserve identity/access boundaries and a consistent hire/start handoff.

## 5. How would you prioritize multiple hiring managers?

Use approved role criticality, operational deadlines, candidate impact and dependencies. Clarify owner and decision needed, record a central queue, batch scheduling, provide candidate updates and escalate blocked approvals rather than leaving candidates without information. Apply consistent standards regardless of candidate background.

## 6. How would you keep recruiting fair and accessible?

Define job-related criteria in advance, use structured evidence, make reasonable accommodations available, train interviewers, retain proper process records and keep voluntary demographic data restricted from decision makers. Any screening algorithm or external vendor requires legal, validity, accessibility and privacy review.

## 7. How do you handle a candidate requesting an accommodation?

Acknowledge the request respectfully, route through authorized HR accommodation channels, preserve confidentiality and provide an accessible alternative under applicable rules. Do not request diagnosis details from the hiring panel or record sensitive medical information in ordinary interview notes.

## 8. What HR KPI would you show an executive?

A balanced view: recruiting demand, filled/open reqs, stage movement, time-to-fill, decided-offer acceptance and candidate-experience survey with response n; on the People Ops side, onboarding due/on-time and HR tickets resolved in policy SLA plus open overdue backlog. Every figure needs a time window, denominator and boundary.

## 9. How would you investigate poor onboarding completion?

Start with due-task exceptions by owner, whether the task was actually due, dependencies on IT/Payroll/HR, and the employee start date. Confirm the authorized process was executed rather than marking a task complete on paper. Implement an owner/approval queue and watch both on-time percent and overdue open count.

## 10. Tell me about your Excel, SQL and Python experience.

Show the formula-linked workbook tabs, SQL constraints + analytical queries, deterministic Python generator, QA tests and two dashboard options. Say clearly that this is a portfolio simulation, and distinguish it from production system administration.

## 11. What would you do if data disagreed across the ATS and HRIS?

Reconcile record grain and canonical IDs, inspect effective dates and status definitions, locate the source-of-truth field, quarantine bad joins, document exception owner, validate corrections and re-run regression checks. Do not overwrite personnel data without authorized approval.

## 12. How should HR handle sensitive cases?

Use minimum necessary information, correct case classification, authorized access, secure specialist routing, approved legal/privacy escalation and a documented resolution. Ordinary support SLAs cannot delay statutory issues.

### Last-mile preparation

Practice explaining one metric definition, one conflict/escalation scenario and one process-improvement hypothesis without reading every line. When discussing outcomes, always separate **what you built and tested** from **what a fictional company might achieve** after implementation.
