# Filled example — 30-day sourcing campaign

**Simulation:** TA Specialist role, REQ-0003, People & Culture. This is a planning artifact; no outreach has been executed and no response/interest numbers are claimed.

## Audience and employer value proposition

Target recruiting operations specialists, HR coordinators with ATS systems exposure, and analysts experienced in hiring process reporting. Emphasize clear role expectations, accessible interviews, work location, approved salary details and growth opportunities where authorized. Avoid protected-class targeting and unnecessary credential proxies.

## Channel plan and work blocks

| Channel | Planned action | Required fields / guardrail | Hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company Careers | Refresh job page and standardized application | job requirements and accessibility contact | Higher self-selection quality |
| LinkedIn | Manual relevant-skills outreach and applicant follow-up | respect opt-out, do not scrape private records | Incremental applicants and interviews |
| Employee Referral | Publish inclusive referral summary | same rubric and process as other sources | Credible candidate introductions |
| University / career centers | Share accessible role description | avoid degree-only exclusion if not job-related | Broader early-career access |
| Professional Network | Review authorized community listings | transparent source attribution | Diversified sourcing |

**Schedule:** weeks 1–2 validate channels and outreach copy; week 3 assess pipeline health and documented feedback; week 4 present applications, interviews and quality/cost data with sample limitations. Compare channels only after controlling for job family/cohort and accounting for source cost.

**Control:** all contact occurs in an approved system with opt-out controls. This repository does not send messages or connect to sourcing platforms.
