# HR service desk triage record — completed fictional exercise

**Case type:** HRIS access • **Employee ID:** synthetic only • **Priority:** Normal • **Case context:** An employee cannot access the approved HRIS self-service portal after a routine system role update. No personal details or credentials are included.

1. **Log:** classify as HRIS Access, record received date and normal priority. Confirm case does not include protected medical or confidential payroll attachments in generic notes.
2. **Authenticate / authorize:** use approved IT identity channel; requester must be authorized. Don't ask for passwords, MFA codes or government identifiers.
3. **Investigate:** check role entitlement in authorized identity system; identify recent change and control owner. Do not grant broad access to bypass policy.
4. **Resolve:** request approved least-privilege role repair through IT/IAM; confirm user can complete required HRIS action; capture closure timestamp and neutral resolution code.
5. **Follow up:** record category and turnaround time. If overdue, escalate via HR Ops queue. Repeat incidents create a root-cause review, not a hidden workaround.

**Metric linkage:** resolved within 5 *calendar* days / all resolved tickets; this sample policy is an internal fictional SLA, not a legal requirement.
