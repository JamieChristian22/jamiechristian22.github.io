# New-hire 30 / 60 / 90 day checklist (editable role template)

| When | Milestone / evidence | Owner | State in completed fictional example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Before start | Equipment and least-privilege access plan | IT | Demonstration: scheduled |
| Day 1 | Welcome, organizational chart, role responsibilities | Manager | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 1–3 | HRIS, payroll, benefits information handoffs | HR Ops | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 1–3 | Authorized I-9 process, no ID docs in this repository | HR Compliance | Demonstration: route only |
| Day 5 | Candidate data handling, interview accessibility and selection training | TA Lead / Legal | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 14 | Own supervised requisition stages and candidate update queue | TA Lead | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 30 | Explain funnel / offer rate / time-to-fill with correct denominator | Recruiter / Analytics | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 60 | Run recruitment meeting agenda and escalation log | TA Ops | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 60 | Correct reporting discrepancies with documented QA | Analytics | Demonstration: planned |
| Day 90 | Present improvement case with evidence and candidate safeguards | TA Lead | Demonstration: planned |

**Success criteria:** clear ownership, timely new-hire experience, responsible data handling, reliable operational reporting. This is a sample, not a completion record.
