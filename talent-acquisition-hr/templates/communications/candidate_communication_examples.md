# Candidate communication library (filled fictional messages; **not sent**)

All examples reference a fictional Northstar role and use no personal names or email addresses. Production communications must use approved ATS workflows, accessibility support, lawful disclosures and actual manager-confirmed dates. These samples demonstrate structure, not real offers or commitments.

We apologize for the confusion. We have corrected the ATS workflow record and will provide the next confirmed update through the approved application channel.

**Safety:** No real candidate outreach is initiated by these files. Never use a portfolio script to automate messages without authorization, consent, accessibility review and legal approval.
